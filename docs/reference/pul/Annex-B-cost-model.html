Annex B to the feedback · parametric model · 7 discussion points · monthly figures

What permissive handling costs, and what stricter handling costs the business

For each of the seven discussion points the model computes two values: expected losses if the Framework is implemented as drafted, and the business cost of our stricter proposal — lost sales, abandoned purchases, operating costs. All parameters are adjustable; results update instantly.

This is a model, not a forecast. Default values are order-of-magnitude assumptions. Actual rates of errors, declines and disputes can only come from pilot operation. The purpose is to show which parameters drive the outcome, and where stricter handling pays off and where it does not.

Summary

Losses per the Framework are expected damages and dispute handling costs; the cost of strictness is lost revenue of the system and merchants’ margin on lost sales, plus operating costs. Turnover is shown separately and not converted to revenue. Inputs are held in roubles; the currency switch converts all displayed amounts at the rate shown. Comment codes C01–C07 correspond to rows 01–07 of the enclosed feedback form.
